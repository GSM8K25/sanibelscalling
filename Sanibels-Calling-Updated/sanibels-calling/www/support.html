Common questions

Where is my shell log? On your phone only. There’s no account and no cloud sync. In the iPhone app a copy is kept in Files › On My iPhone › Sanibel’s Calling.

New phone? Restoring your iPhone backup brings your log along. Or use Shell log › My shell stats › Save backup, send the file to yourself, and Restore backup on the new phone.

Tide times or weather missing? They need a signal. Once loaded, they stay available for a few hours.

Map or photos blank? Maps and photos need a signal the first time; areas and photos you’ve already viewed keep working offline.

Distances look wrong? Go to Tips › Distances and choose “Use my location” or “Save where I’m staying.”

Leave live shells: in Lee County you may not keep any shell with a living animal inside, except oysters, hard clams, sunray venus and coquinas (fishing license required). FWC shell rules
