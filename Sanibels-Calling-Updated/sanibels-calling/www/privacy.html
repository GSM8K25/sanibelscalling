Sanibel’s Calling

Privacy policy · Effective October 22, 2026
The short version: no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your shell log, photos and saved places stay on your phone, and we never see them.

What stays on your phone

Your shell walks, shell counts, notes, marked spots, walking routes, photos and your “My list” of saved places are stored only on your device. In the iPhone app, a copy of your shell log is also kept in the app’s own folder on your phone (visible in the Files app) so it survives if the app’s storage is cleared. That folder is included in your regular iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) if you have backups turned on; Apple handles those backups under your Apple account.

Location

With your permission, the app uses your location while it’s open to show distances, tag a shell walk with the beach you’re on, mark spots and record your walking route if you turn that on. It never tracks you in the background, and your location is never sent to us or to any other service. It is saved only in your shell log on your phone.

Services the app contacts

  • Tides: NOAA Tides & Currents, for tide predictions at a fixed station near Sanibel.
  • Weather: Open-Meteo, for wind and temperature at a fixed point on Sanibel.
  • Photos: Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons, for shell and landmark pictures.
  • Maps: Esri and OpenStreetMap map tiles for the area you’re viewing; Photon (komoot) to place listings on the map from their street addresses.
  • Guide updates: our own website, to download the latest tips and listings.

None of these requests include your location, your shell log or anything that identifies you. Like any internet request, each service sees your device’s IP address as part of the connection, and their own privacy policies apply to that.

Booking and other links

“Check dates & book” and other links open websites outside the app, such as our booking site, Apple Maps or Google Maps. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Sharing

Nothing leaves your phone unless you choose to share it, for example a walk summary, your list, a backup file or a spreadsheet sent through the share sheet.

Deleting your data

Delete a walk or photo in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes its data from your phone, including the backup folder. Copies you shared elsewhere are yours to delete.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
