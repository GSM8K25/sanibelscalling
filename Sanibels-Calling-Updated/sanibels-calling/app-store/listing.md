Sanibel’s Calling is a free guide to Sanibel and Captiva from people who love the islands: where and when to find shells, what you just picked up, where to watch the sun rise and set, and the places locals keep going back to.

FIND MORE SHELLS
• Shelling outlook: the next low tides (with negative lows flagged), wind and moon phase, all in island time
• Shell hotspots with the best time to go and the shells to look for
• A step-by-step shell-finding strategy: tides, fronts, west winds, where the shell line forms, and technique

KNOW WHAT YOU FOUND
• A guide to 39 Sanibel shells, from lightning whelks and fighting conchs to the famous junonia
• “Help me identify a shell”: filter by shape and color
• How to tell each one apart from its look-alikes, and where and when it turns up
• Names checked against the Bailey-Matthews National Shell Museum’s Southwest Florida shell guide

KEEP A SHELL LOG
• Start a walk and the app notes the beach, low tide and wind
• Tap + as you find shells, add photos and special finds, and mark shell piles on the map
• Record your walking route while the app is open
• A life list and stats: see which beaches and conditions work best for you
• Your log stays private on your phone, with an automatic backup in the Files app

EXPLORE THE ISLANDS
• Must-sees, sunrise and sunset spots, and local favorites

• Island tips: parking, the causeway toll, sea turtle season, the stingray shuffle and more
• Save places to My list and share it with your travel crew

## App Privacy (the “nutrition label”)
Choose **“No, we do not collect data from this app.”** The shell log, photos and saved places stay on the device. Tides and weather are requested for fixed island coordinates, never the user’s location, and nothing is sent to you.

## App Review notes (paste into “Notes” under App Review Information)
Sanibel’s Calling is a free visitor guide and shelling logbook for Sanibel and Captiva Islands, Florida. There is no login or account, and personal data (the shell log) is stored only on the device.

To try it: Explore shows tips, must-sees and sunrise/sunset spots. The Shells tab shows tides and the shell guide (tap any shell). On the Shell log tab, tap “Start a shell walk” (allow location; it’s used to name the beach and mark spots), then “+ Pick shells to count” and tap + to count shells. The Map tab shows places and shell hotspots.

The “Check dates & book” button opens our own vacation rental booking website in Safari. The app itself sells nothing and has no in-app purchases.
